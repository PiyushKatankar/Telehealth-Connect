Why TeleHealth Connect

Everything a real clinic needs to schedule

Not a mock-up: every feature below runs on the live Flask backend you can explore in this repo.

Live slot availability

Slots update over WebSocket the instant someone books — no refresh, no stale calendars, no race conditions.

Transaction-safe booking

Bookings run inside a SQL transaction with row-level locking plus a database constraint, so a slot can only ever be claimed once.

Symptom chat

After booking, chat directly with the doctor in a private room so symptoms are documented before the visit starts.

Role-based access

Patients book and message; doctors publish availability and manage their schedule. Permissions are enforced server-side.

How it works

From “I need a doctor” to confirmed — in three steps

1

Create your free account

Sign up as a patient in seconds — or as a doctor, if you want to publish availability and receive patients.

2

Pick any open slot

Choose a doctor and a time from their live calendar. If someone grabs a slot while you are choosing, it disappears instantly.

3

Chat before your visit

Your booking opens a private chat with the doctor, so symptoms and questions are ready before the appointment starts.
